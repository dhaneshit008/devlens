# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome contributors with different experience
levels and backgrounds. Critique code and evidence, not personal worth. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, publishing private information and sustained personal attacks
are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or ban repeat offenders.
Responses should be proportional, documented where appropriate, and respect privacy.
Contact the lead maintainer through their GitHub profile for a private reporting channel;
do not post sensitive details in public issues. Good-faith reports will be handled fairly.
