# Governance

Dhanesh M V is the creator and lead maintainer. The lead maintainer stewards architecture,
reviews releases, handles sensitive reports and makes final decisions when consensus
cannot be reached. Technical decisions should cite evidence, tests and practical tradeoffs.

Contributors can become maintainers through sustained useful contributions, thoughtful
review and dependable community conduct. Access is granted as needed and can be removed
when responsibilities change. No employer, sponsorship or contribution-count threshold
confers automatic control. Licensing changes require explicit maintainer authorization.
